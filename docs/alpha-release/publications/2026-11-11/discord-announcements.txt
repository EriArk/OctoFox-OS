## TrainerOS — the first public alpha is here! 😁

Well, this project got a little out of hand.

Games, multiplayer, friends, apps and everything around them — I've been trying to bring it all into one connected gaming environment. And you can finally try it!

Create a **Lobby**, invite friends, choose a game and get ready together. Keep talking through messages and voice while you play — even when everyone is playing something different.

There's your own **3D character and wardrobe**, **Crates** for games, apps and widgets, and an early **SDK** for connecting more projects to the same world. Plus the browser, files, Quick Apps, achievements and a lot of other things that somehow found their way into this project. 😁

This is **Alpha 7**, currently for Odin 2 and Flip 2 with ArmadaOS. It's an alpha, so try what interests you and tell me what works, what breaks and what you'd like to see next. Successful experiments are useful too!
