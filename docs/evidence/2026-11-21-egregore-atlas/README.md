# Flying the Egregore atlas

These frames were taken on Odin 2 from Ship Home `cb7e53363b826caf` in the mobile renderer. The avionics circuit used software-generated gamepad and touch input, including one-finger orbit and two-finger zoom. The flight check measured 174.5 seconds of simulated full-throttle cruise.

The field layout and drive range use schematic atlas units. Positions inside a cluster, including the altitude of each contact, use the same metre coordinates as the flight model. The energy ring surrounding a single Source follows the project's [cosmology](https://github.com/EriArk/AbyssWorldGame/blob/main/docs/COSMOLOGY.md). The three navigable clusters and their coordinates are specific to Ship Home.

The flight timing check takes off from Echo Orchard, climbs clear, points toward Table Isle and runs the normal cruise physics at full throttle. It measures from engaging cruise to the approach, 190 metres short of the point above the destination pad. The departure climb, braking and final descent are separate. The route test rejects a direct Lantern–Cinder crossing, checks the fuel minimum at the intermediate stop and verifies that the second arrival acquires Cinder Garden as the landing destination.
