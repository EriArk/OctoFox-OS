🦊 **TrainerOS is now OctoFox OS — Alpha 9 is out!**

Hey everyone!

Well, I had to rename TrainerOS because the name was already taken.

So, meet **OctoFox OS!** Same project, new name, and quite a lot has changed along the way.

🎮 **What’s OctoFox OS?**

My attempt to turn Linux handhelds into a complete gaming system, where your games, friends, achievements, apps, and multiplayer all belong together.

✨ **What’s been happening?**

* **Games & Emulation** — A unified library, collections, different browsing layouts, emulator integration, and in-game menus.
* **Multiplayer & Social** — Play retro games with friends online without the usual emulator headaches. No messing with IP addresses, ports, or complicated netplay setups for supported games. Just create a Lobby, invite your friends, pick a game, and play. Plus integrated chats, voice calls, and communities.
* **Personal Profiles** — Customizable 3D avatars, achievements, progression, and deeper integrations with supported games.
