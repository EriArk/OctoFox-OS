## 🛠️ OctoFox OS
*Formerly TrainerOS — renamed on 13 November 2026. The dated overview below is retained; see the latest posts for Alpha 7.*
An Armada-based OS for compatible Linux handhelds.

I wanted my handheld to feel like a complete console. Your games, friends and progress stay together, and supported Adventures bring parts of the game into the OS even after you stop playing.

### 📍 Status
**Active Development · preparing the first public release**
Running on Odin 2 and Retroid Pocket Flip 2; source builds are available, but there's no ready-to-flash image yet.

### ✨ Features
✅ Browse games by series, launch them and return to your library
✅ Messages, groups and voice through Fluxer
✅ Invite friends to supported games, online or on nearby handhelds
✅ In the first Adventure: progress, party and storage, healing, shops, nearby trades and friendly battles
🚧 More multiplayer games and testing across different networks
🚧 Completing online Adventure activities
⬜ Game-specific Adventures for more series
⬜ An installable image and system updates

✅ Available in current builds · 🚧 In development · ⬜ Planned
Game and device support varies; group voice and multiplayer testing are still ongoing.
