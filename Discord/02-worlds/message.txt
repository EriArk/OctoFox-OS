*Just to clarify: OctoFox OS is not a traditional emulator frontend or a standalone OS image. It’s a complete gaming environment that installs on top of ArmadaOS, integrating game management, system features, social services, applications, and more into a unified experience. ArmadaOS remains the underlying operating system.*

*This is still an early release, not a finished replacement for your existing setup.*
